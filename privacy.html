PomoTime Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Privacy Policy Terms of Service
Android & iOS Mobile Application

1. Introduction

Welcome to PomoTime ("we", "our", "us"), a Pomodoro timer, mindful recovery, and productivity application for Android and iOS developed by SLaboratory. We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing data collection.

This Privacy Policy explains how PomoTime handles local data on your device and limited diagnostic and subscription data processed through third-party infrastructure providers. By using PomoTime, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

2. Information We Collect & Store

No Account Required: PomoTime does not require user registration, passwords, email addresses, or a cloud account on SLaboratory servers.

A. Local Data Storage & User-Initiated Backups

All core productivity data is stored strictly in a local database on your device and is never uploaded to SLaboratory servers:

  • Focus timer presets, active session progress, and notification sound preferences.
  • Tasks, categories/tags, recurring schedules, and reminders.
  • Break notes, productivity history, streaks, achievements, and health tip display history.
  • Visual appearance preferences (language, light/dark mode, skin selection, and customizations).
  • Optional Local Backups: If you enable internal backups or export a backup file, a JSON copy of your local data is created on your device. If you choose to share or save an exported backup file to an external folder or third-party cloud drive, that file is governed by the privacy policy of your chosen storage provider.

B. Device Permissions (Processed On-Device Only)

Depending on your operating system and settings, PomoTime requests permissions strictly to run core features locally on your device:

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms: To alert you reliably when work intervals, breaks, or scheduled task reminders complete.
  • Foreground Service & Vibration (Android): To keep active timers, live countdown notifications, and focus audio running smoothly in the background.

PomoTime does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos library, or precise GPS location.

C. Diagnostics, Analytics & Subscription Infrastructure

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or sell data to advertisers. We use the following trusted providers solely to maintain app stability, understand aggregate usage, and verify Premium purchases:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): Collects anonymous, aggregated usage events (such as timer completions, feature usage, and language changes) along with standard app-instance identifiers to help us improve usability. No personal task titles or break notes are sent to analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): Collects technical crash logs, stack traces, operating system version, and device model metadata if the app crashes or encounters unexpected errors.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.): Processes purchase receipts, transaction tokens, entitlement status, and a randomly generated anonymous app-user ID through Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple StoreKit (iOS) to unlock and restore Premium access.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by PomoTime is used solely to:

  • Provide core timer, breathing break, audio, task scheduling, and analytics features on your device.
  • Verify, maintain, and restore Premium subscriptions or one-time unlocks across reinstalls.
  • Diagnose crashes and resolve software defects via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Understand aggregate feature adoption to guide product improvements via Firebase Analytics.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Data is shared only with the infrastructure providers listed in Section 2 (Google Firebase and RevenueCat) and platform app stores (Google Play and Apple App Store) as strictly necessary for app stability and billing verification, or where required by applicable law.

5. Data Retention & Your Controls

You remain in full control of your local data inside the App:

  • Delete All Local Data: In the Account tab inside PomoTime, you can use Delete all app data to erase timers, tasks, notes, and statistics, or Delete internal backup files to wipe automatic local backups.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the App removes the local app database from your device (subject to any OS-level device backup or manually exported files you keep).
  • System Privacy & Subscriptions: You can revoke notification permissions in your Android or iOS system settings, and manage subscriptions through Google Play or your Apple ID settings.

6. Children's Privacy

PomoTime is a general productivity tool not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age for digital consent in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in certain EEA member states). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

7. International Users (GDPR / EEA, UK & Regional Rights)

If you reside in the EEA, UK, or another region with data protection laws (such as GDPR), you may have rights regarding access, rectification, restriction, objection, or deletion of personal data. We process limited diagnostic and billing metadata based on legitimate interests (maintaining app reliability and security) and contractual necessity (delivering paid Premium entitlements).

Because SLaboratory does not maintain user accounts or store personal profiles on its own servers, most data resides directly on your device. For any privacy inquiry, please reach us at the contact address below.
